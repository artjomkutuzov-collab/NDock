# Bundled components

NDock bundles CPython 3.12.16 for Apple Silicon from Astral's [python-build-standalone](https://github.com/astral-sh/python-build-standalone), runtime release 20261026.

The runtime's provenance record and license texts are included in NDock.app under `Contents/Resources/python/RUNTIME_PROVENANCE.json` and `Contents/Resources/python/licenses/`. These notices describe the bundled components; they do not grant rights to the NDock application itself.
